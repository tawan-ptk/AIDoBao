  Data Dictionary
Column
Description
ID
Number of questionnaire (Responder’s number)
Diabetes
Diabetic status of the responder (1 refers to having diabetes, 0 refers to not having the disease)
Sex
Gender/sex of the responder (1 refers to men, 2 refers to women)
Age (yr)
The responder’s age in years
Weight (kg)
Weight of the responder in kilograms
Height (cm)
Height of the responder in centimeters
Hip (cm)
Hip circumference of the responder in centimeters
BMI (kg/m2)
Body Mass Index value of the responder (weight in kg/height in m2)
BMI_high
Responder with a high BMI or those who have a BMI of 25.0 kg/m2 or higher (1 refers to those with BMI of 25 kg/m2or more, 0 refers to those with BMI that is lower than 25.0 kg/m2
WHR
Waist to hip ratio (WHR) of the responder
WHR_high
Responder with a high WHR (1 refers to those with WHR of 0.9 or more for men or 0.85 or more for women, 0 refers those with WHR less than 0.9 for men or less than 0.85 for women)
WC (cm)
Waist circumference of the responder in centimeters
WC_high
Responder with a high WC (1 refers to those with WC of 90 cm or more for men or 80 cm or more for women, 0 refers to those with WC lower than 90 cm for men or WC lower than 80 cm for women)
Occup_gr
Occupation of the responder (1 refers to retired, housewife or unemployed, 2 refers to farmers, 3 refers to business owners or civil servants, 4 refers to general employees)
LifeStyle 
Lifestyle of the responder (1 refers to an evening person, 2 refers to a morning person)
Eveningperson
Responder who has an evening lifestyle (1 refers to those who are evening persons, 0 refers to those who are not evening persons)
Sleephour (h)
Number of hours that the responder usually sleeps per day
Sleephour_gr
Sleep hours group (1 refers to those who sleep for 6-7 hours, 2 refers to those who sleep for 5 hours or less, 3 refers to those who sleep for 8 hours or more per day)
LongSleeper
Responder who sleeps for 8 h or more (long sleeper) 
(1 refers to long sleepers, 0 refers to not long sleepers)
ShortSleeper
Responder who sleeps for 5 h or less (short sleeper) 
(1 refers to short sleepers, 0 refers to not short sleepers)
PoorSleeper
Responder who is either a long sleeper or short sleeper (poor sleeper) (1 refers to poor sleepers, 0 refers to not poor sleepers)
CigSmoker
Responder who has smoked more than 100 cigarettes over their lifetime (cigarette smoker) (1 refers to cigarette smokers, 0 refers to not cigarette smokers)
AlcoholUser
Responder who drinks alcohol 2-4 times or more per week (alcohol user)  (1 refers to alcohol users, 0 refers to not alcohol users)
